Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our application ("the App") handles user information. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the services used within the App.

Information Collection and Use

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number for registration.

However, the App uses third-party Google services, including Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob, which may automatically collect certain information to provide analytics, improve app performance, and display advertisements.

Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality and performance.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as:

  • App usage and interaction data.
  • Device information and operating system details.
  • App performance and diagnostic information.
  • App session and engagement information.
  • Other analytics information as configured in the App.

This information helps us understand app usage patterns and improve the overall user experience.

Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Google AdMob may collect certain information, including advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and ad interaction information, to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on applicable settings, user consent, and regional requirements, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information about how Google uses information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

Data Storage

The App does not maintain its own database of users' personal information.

Information processed by Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob may be handled and stored by Google according to their respective privacy policies and service terms.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics: Used to understand app usage and improve app performance. Firebase Privacy Information .
  • Google AdMob: Used to display and measure advertisements. AdMob Privacy Information .

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control their independent data processing practices.

Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Security

We value user privacy and take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.
