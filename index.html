Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how our application (“the App”) operates and clarifies that we do not collect, store, or share personal information from users.

Information Collection and Use

The App does not require user accounts and does not collect, store, or retain any personally identifiable information.

Any values provided by the user within the App are used only temporarily to retrieve publicly available information from external sources and are not saved, logged, or transmitted to our own servers.

Data Storage

The App does not maintain its own database of user data. No information entered or viewed in the App is stored locally or remotely after the request is completed.

Third-Party Services

The App may display information obtained from publicly accessible third-party services. We do not control how those external services manage data, and users are encouraged to review their respective privacy policies.

The App itself does not share user information with third parties.

Children’s Privacy

This App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Security

We value user privacy and take reasonable measures to ensure that the App does not expose or misuse any information during normal operation.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the App indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through the developer contact information provided on the app store listing.
